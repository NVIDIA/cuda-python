48class CUDAWarning(RuntimeWarning): 

49 """Warning issued when ``cuda.core`` hits a CUDA error it cannot raise. 

50  

51 ``cuda.core`` raises exceptions for failures in ordinary calls. Some failures 

52 happen where no exception can propagate: while a resource is released by the 

53 garbage collector or by a CUDA callback, including the driver calls that 

54 switch and restore the CUDA context around such a release. Those failures 

55 are reported as this warning instead, and the affected resource may have 

56 leaked. 

57  

58 Filter on this category to make such failures fatal in tests:: 

59  

60 warnings.filterwarnings("error", category=cuda.core.CUDAWarning) 

61  

62 Because the report comes from a destructor, an escalated warning cannot be 

63 raised into user code; it is delivered through :func:`sys.unraisablehook` 

64 (which pytest surfaces as ``PytestUnraisableExceptionWarning``). 

65  

66 .. versionadded:: 1.3.0 

67 """
